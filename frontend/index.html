Full Assumptions & Methodology

Investment (Renter)

  • Benchmark: 60% equities, 40% bonds (e.g., S&P 500 + bond index)
  • Annual Return: 5.5% nominal (historical average)
  • Compounding: Monthly
  • Investable Funds: Down payment + any positive monthly savings (if renting is cheaper than owning)
  • Tax Treatment: Long-term capital gains tax applied at sale (~15–20% depending on income bracket)

Home Purchase (Buyer)

  • Down Payment: You specify as a % of purchase price
  • Mortgage: Amortized deterministically based on rate and term
  • Costs: Property tax (annual %), insurance (annual $), maintenance (annual % of price)
  • Appreciation: Annual rate (you specify; default 3%). Sale price = Purchase × (1 + appreciation)^years
  • Selling Costs: 6% of sale price (realtor commission + fees)
  • Tax Benefit: Mortgage interest is deductible; property tax is deductible

Comparison Methodology

  • Buyer Outcome: Sale proceeds (price - selling costs - remaining mortgage balance) = equity at sale
  • Renter Outcome: Down payment and monthly surplus invested at 5.5% annual return = portfolio value
  • Verdict: Which scenario leaves you with more wealth after the holding period?

Key Limitations & Simplifications

  • No inflation adjustment (all figures in today's dollars)
  • Deterministic projections (no Monte Carlo / scenario analysis)
  • Assumes you have funds available to pay rent or own; no credit constraints modeled
  • Property-specific depreciation (e.g., roof replacement) not modeled in detail
  • Rents assumed to grow at inflation rate (3% by default, not editable in this version)
  • No state-specific tax rules or incentives (simplified federal model)
